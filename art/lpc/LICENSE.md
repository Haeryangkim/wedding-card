## Licence

Every image is licensed per file (upstream `CREDITS.csv`, same commit). The upstream README says: "Each piece of artwork distributed from this project (all images in the `spritesheets` subdirectory) is licensed under one or more of the following supported open license(s)": CC0, CC-BY-SA, CC-BY, OGA-BY, GPL, and "you must at least credit all the authors (except for CC0 licensed artwork)". The credits must be "accessible from within your game or app and can be reasonably discovered by users".

The download is split in two folders by licence:

- `raw/spritesheets/` (180 files): every file offers a non-share-alike option (CC0, OGA-BY 3.0 or CC-BY 3.0). Use these under that option: credit required (except CC0), no share-alike.
- `raw/spritesheets-sa/` (29 files): offered ONLY under CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0 (no permissive alternative exists upstream for these items): collared suit jacket, waistcoat, necktie, top hat, bowler hat, tiara, round glasses, sunglasses. If any of these is shipped, the composed sprites and any edited copies of those sheets must be offered under CC-BY-SA 3.0 (this covers the art, not the app code), and the CC-BY-SA anti-DRM clause applies. Everything in this folder is optional: a groom can be built without it (dress shirt + bow tie + trousers) and the bride does not need the tiara.

Licence used per file: CC-BY 3.0: 2, CC-BY-SA 3.0: 29, CC0: 22, OGA-BY 3.0: 144, OGA-BY 3.0+: 12
